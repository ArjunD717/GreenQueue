Three moves, one quiet window.

GreenQueue is an event-driven AWS build. Submission is synchronous; execution is deferred to the cleanest half-hour the forecast allows. Every choice is visible in the queue.

01

Submit

A Lambda validates the job and asks the shared planner for the best feasible window.

02

Forecast

The planner scans 96 half-hour slots, weighing every window that ends before the deadline.

03

Dispatch

EventBridge ticks every minute. Due jobs move from queued to running, then complete.
